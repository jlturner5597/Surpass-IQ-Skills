01. What is a Skill?

A Skill is a structured instruction set — typically a system prompt or workflow guide — that configures Surpass IQ to perform a specific task exceptionally well. Skills encapsulate expert knowledge about how to use Surpass IQ for a particular use case, so others can benefit without having to figure it out from scratch.

For example, a skill might teach Surpass IQ to author clinical vignette MCQs using established best practices, or to structure a tag hierarchy for a specific discipline.

02. What makes a good skill?

  • Focused on a single use case — it does one thing well, rather than trying to cover everything.
  • Includes clear instructions — the skill file tells Surpass IQ exactly what to do, in what format, and with what constraints.
  • Generalisable — useful to a broader audience, not just your specific institution or subject area.
  • Tested — you've used it yourself and it produces consistent, high-quality results.

Read the Contributing Guide for full formatting instructions and the expected file structure.

03. The review process

  1. Submit. Complete the Google Form with your skill title, category, description, and the full skill content.
  2. Review. A member of the Surpass team reads and tests your skill, usually within 5 working days.
  3. Feedback. If changes are needed, we'll get in touch via the email address you provide. Most skills are approved with minor edits.
  4. Published. Approved skills are formatted, added to the repository, and appear on this site automatically within minutes of merging.

By submitting a skill, you agree that it may be published under the community licence and credited to you (or your organisation) as specified in your submission.
